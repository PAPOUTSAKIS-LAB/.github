# Papoutsakis Lab

**Systems & Synthetic Biological Engineering** · Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering, University of Delaware

We engineer cells for a sustainable, healthier future, using both experiments and computation. Led by Prof. Eleftherios "Terry" Papoutsakis.

## Research

**🦠 Microbial Biotechnology & Synthetic Biology**
- Carbon-negative fermentation: syntrophic clostridial co-cultures that fix CO₂ into fuels and chemicals
- Genomics and regulatory networks: RNA-seq, ¹³C-metabolic flux analysis, genome-editing tools for *Clostridium*
- Stress response, sporulation and synthetic methylotrophy in *E. coli*
- Microbial interactions, extracellular vesicles and microbiomes

**🧬 Cell & Gene-Therapy Bioengineering**
- Hematopoietic stem-cell bioengineering
- Megakaryocytes, platelets and megakaryocytic extracellular vesicles (MkEVs) for cargo delivery
- Cell-culture biomanufacturing and bioreactor modeling

## What's here

Code from the group's research: analysis pipelines, modeling and flux tools, sequencing workflows and lab utilities. Public repos usually go with a publication. If you use one, please cite the paper linked in that repo.
